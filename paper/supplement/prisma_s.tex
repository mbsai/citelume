\section*{Supplementary Material S1. PRISMA-S checklist}

The searches reported here are those \textsc{CiteLume} ran for each of the 22 test reviews (and 11 development
reviews) in the evaluation, as stored in each run's corpus. Item numbers follow PRISMA-S.

\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.5cm}p{3.6cm}X@{}}
\toprule
\# & Item & Where and how reported \\
\midrule
1 & Database name & PubMed (NCBI E-utilities) with Europe PMC as its mirror; Semantic Scholar with OpenAlex as a
fallback; bioRxiv/medRxiv (through Europe PMC); arXiv. Methods, Section 2.2. \\
2 & Multi-database searching & Each database searched separately with its own compiled string; not searched on one
platform. Methods 2.2. \\
3 & Study registries & None searched. Limitations. \\
4 & Online resources and browsing & None. \\
5 & Citation searching & Forward and backward citation searching of up to 60 selected studies through Semantic
Scholar (records linked to at least 2 added), and the reference lists of on-topic reviews set aside at eligibility.
Methods 2.2; counts in each flow diagram. \\
6 & Contacts & None. \\
7 & Other methods & None. \\
8 & Full search strategies & Compiled by fixed rules from the question (review title); every string run, for every
database and review, is stored in the run's corpus in the evaluation archive (S3) and shown with each result. An
example is given below the table. \\
9 & Limits and restrictions & Publication year up to the year of the review version used, added to each PubMed
string as \texttt{AND (1900:YYYY[dp])}; no language limit. Methods 3.2. \\
10 & Search filters & A reviews search with review and meta-analysis publication-type filters; no validated
study-design filter for trials. Methods 2.2. \\
11 & Prior work & Strings are not reused from earlier reviews; Cochrane's own strategies were not used. \\
12 & Updates & Not applicable (single search per run). \\
13 & Dates of searches & 18--24 November 2026; the date and time of every run are stored in its run record. \\
14 & Peer review & The compiled strings were not peer reviewed (PRESS); they are rule-generated and published. \\
15 & Total records & Per review and per database in each corpus and flow diagram (S3). \\
16 & Deduplication & Automatic, by DOI, PubMed ID, arXiv ID or normalised title (Methods 2.3); every merge is stored
and replayable. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
