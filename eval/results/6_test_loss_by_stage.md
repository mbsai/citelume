**Largest loss: search.**

Of the 435 included studies no search returned, 266 are matched by at least one of CiteLume's own PubMed queries when it is run without a result limit (a search-depth loss); the rest are matched by none of them (a query-sensitivity loss).
Other methods (citation chasing, reference lists of reviews) found 15 included studies that no database search returned.
