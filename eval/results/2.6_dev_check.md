## Reading the comparison (26 Nov 2026)

- Single runs of each version, so differences of one or two studies are within the run-to-run variation of the
  sources (Session 7, E2c). The PubMed queries are what the protocol changes; the other sources vary by availability.
- **Gains that come from 2.6:** CD012455 (*melatonin; sleep; intensive care unit*) 1 to 5 of 5 found and selected,
  from the new core search of the main concept with the third concept; CD008018 3 to 5 selected and CD010355 2 to 3
  found, from the same.
- **CD010038 (3 to 0) is not the protocol:** both versions sent identical PubMed queries and got the same 767 PubMed
  records; the 2.6 run lost records because Semantic Scholar did not answer in time (100 against 380 records) and
  bioRxiv did not answer.
- **A first version of 2.6 was narrowed before release:** it kept every word before a mapped head noun required
  with the heading, and on CD012223 ("refractory glaucoma") cut the records from 248 to 22 (3 to 1 found). It now
  keeps only a substance (a PubMed Supplementary Concept, such as atrazine); CD012223 is back to 251 records, 3 found.
  The first version's runs are kept in runs/26check_v26_old.
- No development review lost included studies because of 2.6; no test review was run with it.
