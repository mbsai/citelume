## Reading the comparison (26 Nov 2026)

- **OpenAlex is not measured here.** Run locally without an OpenAlex key, every OpenAlex search was refused: the
  shared keyless daily budget had been spent by earlier test runs that day (27 refusals in the run log; 0 OpenAlex
  records in every corpus). It needs a run with a key (the demo has one) or after the budget resets.
- **ClinicalTrials.gov** returned records for 9 of the 11 reviews (2 to 82 per review) and contributed one included
  study that no other source found (CD012551, chronic prostatitis).
- The other differences are the sources' run-to-run variation, not the protocol: CD008018 (10 to 7) received the
  same 822 PubMed records in both versions and nothing from the registry; CD007868 (0 to 2) gained its studies from
  other sources.
- No development review lost included studies because of 2.7.
